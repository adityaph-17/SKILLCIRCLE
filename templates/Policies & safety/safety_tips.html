Safety Tips

Simple steps to help you stay safe while using SkillCircle.

Last updated: October 2026

1. Protect Your Account

Keep your SkillCircle account secure at all times.

  • Use a strong and unique password.
  • Never share your password with another person.
  • Do not use the same password on multiple websites.
  • Log out when using a shared or public computer.
  • Contact SkillCircle if you notice suspicious account activity.

2. Protect Personal Information

Be careful about the information you share with other users.

  • Do not share your password.
  • Do not share bank or payment details unnecessarily.
  • Do not publish government identification numbers.
  • Avoid sharing your exact home address publicly.
  • Do not share private information about other people.

3. Check User Profiles

Before interacting with someone, review the information available on their SkillCircle profile.

  • Check their listed skills and experience.
  • Look for consistent and reasonable profile information.
  • Be careful if someone provides suspicious or contradictory information.
  • Do not assume that a profile automatically proves professional qualifications.

4. Be Careful With Links and Files

Do not open links or download files from unknown users unless you trust the source.

  • Do not provide your password through an unknown link.
  • Do not download suspicious files.
  • Be careful with links asking for money or personal information.
  • Report suspicious messages or content.

5. Avoid Scams

Be cautious if someone asks you for money or makes unrealistic promises.

Never send money, passwords, verification codes, or sensitive information to someone simply because they contacted you through SkillCircle.
  • Be careful with urgent payment requests.
  • Do not trust guaranteed income or unrealistic offers.
  • Verify information before making any payment.
  • Report suspected scams.

6. Meeting Someone Offline

If you decide to meet another SkillCircle user in person, take reasonable precautions.

  • Meet in a public and familiar place.
  • Meet during reasonable daytime or public hours.
  • Tell a trusted person where you are going.
  • Share the person's name and meeting location with someone you trust.
  • Keep your phone available.
  • Arrange your own transportation when possible.
  • Leave immediately if you feel uncomfortable or unsafe.

7. Do Not Share Your Exact Home Address

Avoid giving your exact home address to someone you have only recently met online.

If an offline skill session is necessary, consider using a public or appropriate location instead.

8. Trust Your Instincts

You do not have to continue an interaction that makes you uncomfortable.

You can stop communicating, decline a request, block a user where the feature is available, or report the user.

9. Keep Learning Activities Safe

Some skills may involve tools, equipment, physical activities, chemicals, electrical systems, or other risks.

Before participating in such activities:

  • Understand the possible risks.
  • Use appropriate safety equipment.
  • Follow proper instructions.
  • Do not attempt activities beyond your ability.
  • Seek qualified professional guidance when necessary.

10. Protect Minors and Vulnerable People

Take additional care when an interaction involves children or vulnerable individuals.

Do not arrange unsafe private meetings or inappropriate interactions with minors.

11. Report Suspicious Behaviour

Report users or content that appear to violate SkillCircle's rules or create a safety concern.

Examples include:

  • Threats or harassment.
  • Scams or fraudulent requests.
  • Suspicious links or files.
  • Requests for sensitive information.
  • Impersonation.
  • Inappropriate or illegal content.

12. If You Feel Unsafe

If you feel that you are in immediate danger, move to a safe location and contact local emergency services or a trusted person.

SkillCircle is an online platform and cannot replace emergency services or professional assistance.

13. SkillCircle's Role

SkillCircle provides tools for people to connect and share skills. We cannot guarantee the identity, qualifications, intentions, or behaviour of every user.

Stay cautious, protect your personal information, and use your own judgment when interacting with other members.

14. Report Serious Issues

For serious safety concerns, abuse, threats, scams, or other violations, contact the SkillCircle administration through the reporting or contact feature provided on the platform.
