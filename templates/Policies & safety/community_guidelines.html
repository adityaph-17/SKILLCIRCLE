Community Guidelines

Guidelines for creating a respectful, helpful, and safe SkillCircle community.

Last updated: October 2026

1. Our Community

SkillCircle is built around people sharing knowledge, learning new skills, and helping others in their community.

Every member is expected to treat other users with respect and behave responsibly.

2. Be Respectful

Communicate with other members politely and respectfully.

  • Respect different opinions and skill levels.
  • Do not insult or humiliate other users.
  • Do not threaten or intimidate anyone.
  • Do not use abusive or hateful language.
  • Respect personal boundaries.

3. Be Honest

Users should provide truthful information about themselves and the skills they offer.

  • Do not pretend to have skills or qualifications you do not have.
  • Do not impersonate another person.
  • Do not create fake profiles.
  • Do not provide deliberately misleading information.

4. Share Skills Responsibly

When teaching or sharing a skill, provide information honestly and responsibly.

If a skill requires professional qualifications, certification, supervision, or specialist knowledge, users should clearly communicate their level of experience.

SkillCircle does not verify every user's professional qualifications. Users should independently verify qualifications when they matter.

5. Keep Communication Appropriate

Communication on SkillCircle should remain relevant to learning, teaching, skill sharing, and community interaction.

  • Do not send unwanted messages repeatedly.
  • Do not pressure another user to respond.
  • Do not send sexually inappropriate messages.
  • Do not send threatening messages.
  • Do not repeatedly contact someone after they ask you to stop.

6. No Harassment or Bullying

Harassment, bullying, stalking, threats, and intimidation are not allowed.

This includes behaviour that happens through messages, profiles, comments, requests, or offline interactions that started through SkillCircle.

7. No Hate or Discrimination

SkillCircle does not allow content or behaviour that promotes hatred, discrimination, or violence against people or groups.

Users should not target others based on personal characteristics or background.

8. No Scams or Fraud

SkillCircle must not be used to deceive or financially exploit other users.

  • Do not request money through fraudulent claims.
  • Do not create fake opportunities.
  • Do not use another person's identity.
  • Do not share misleading links intended to steal information.
  • Do not attempt to obtain passwords or account details.

9. Protect Personal Information

Respect the privacy of other members.

  • Do not publish another person's private information.
  • Do not share private messages without appropriate permission.
  • Do not ask users for unnecessary sensitive information.
  • Do not attempt to collect personal information from users.

10. Appropriate Content

Content shared on SkillCircle should be suitable for a community learning platform.

Do not upload or share:

  • Illegal content.
  • Malware or harmful files.
  • Explicit sexual content.
  • Content promoting violence or serious harm.
  • Content intended to harass or threaten others.
  • Copyrighted material that you do not have permission to share.
  • Spam or misleading promotional content.

11. Advertising and Promotion

SkillCircle should not be used for excessive advertising, spam, or unsolicited commercial promotion.

If promotional features are introduced in the future, they may be subject to separate rules.

12. Respect Boundaries

Every user has the right to decide who they communicate or interact with.

If another user declines a request or asks you to stop contacting them, respect that decision.

13. Reporting Problems

If you see behaviour or content that violates these guidelines, report it through the available reporting feature or contact the SkillCircle administration.

When making a report, provide accurate information and enough details for the issue to be reviewed.

14. Moderation

SkillCircle may review reports and take appropriate action when these guidelines are violated.

Actions may include:

  • Removing inappropriate content.
  • Restricting certain account features.
  • Issuing warnings.
  • Temporarily suspending an account.
  • Permanently terminating an account.

15. False Reports

Reporting a genuine concern is encouraged. However, users should not intentionally submit false reports to harass, punish, or target another member.

16. Safety Comes First

SkillCircle encourages users to take reasonable precautions when communicating or meeting people they do not know.

Please read our Safety Tips before arranging an offline meeting with another user.

17. Updates to These Guidelines

These Community Guidelines may be updated as SkillCircle develops and new safety or community requirements arise.

The latest version will always be available on this page.

18. Contact

If you have questions about these guidelines or need to report a serious community issue, contact the SkillCircle administration through the contact method provided on the platform.
