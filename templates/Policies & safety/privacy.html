Privacy Policy

This policy explains how SkillCircle collects, uses, stores, and protects user information.

Last updated: October 2026

1. Introduction

SkillCircle respects your privacy and aims to handle your personal information responsibly.

This Privacy Policy explains what information may be collected when you use SkillCircle and how that information may be used.

2. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use the platform, SkillCircle may collect the following information:

  • Name or display name.
  • Email address.
  • Account login information.
  • Profile information.
  • Skills you want to learn.
  • Skills you want to teach or share.
  • Location or general location information provided by you.
  • Profile pictures or other uploaded content.
  • Messages or requests sent through the platform.
  • Reports and feedback submitted by users.

3. Information Collected Automatically

Some technical information may be collected automatically when you use the website.

  • IP address.
  • Browser type.
  • Device information.
  • Operating system.
  • Pages visited.
  • Basic usage and error information.

This information may be used to maintain security, improve performance, and troubleshoot technical problems.

4. How We Use Your Information

SkillCircle may use collected information to:

  • Create and manage your account.
  • Provide SkillCircle features.
  • Help users discover relevant skills and people.
  • Process learning or teaching requests.
  • Send important account-related communications.
  • Improve the platform.
  • Detect and prevent misuse or fraudulent activity.
  • Respond to support requests.
  • Investigate reports and violations.
  • Maintain platform security.
  • Comply with applicable legal requirements.

5. Profile Information

Some information in your profile may be visible to other SkillCircle users.

This may include your name, profile information, skills, learning interests, and general location, depending on the features you use.

Do not publish sensitive personal information such as passwords, financial information, government identity numbers, or your exact home address in your public profile.

6. Location Information

SkillCircle may use location information to help users find relevant skill-sharing opportunities in their area.

Users should avoid sharing their exact home address publicly. Only provide location information that is necessary for using the platform.

7. How We Share Information

SkillCircle does not intend to sell personal information to third parties.

Information may be shared when necessary to:

  • Provide platform functionality.
  • Operate hosting and technical services.
  • Send emails or other required communications.
  • Protect the security of SkillCircle and its users.
  • Investigate violations or abuse.
  • Comply with legal obligations.

8. Third-Party Services

SkillCircle may use third-party services for hosting, email delivery, analytics, maps, authentication, or other technical functions.

These services may process information according to their own privacy policies and terms.

9. Data Security

SkillCircle uses reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect user information.

However, no online service can guarantee complete security. Users should also protect their accounts by using strong passwords and keeping login information private.

10. Passwords and Account Security

Passwords should be kept confidential and should not be shared with other people.

SkillCircle should store passwords using appropriate password-hashing methods rather than storing passwords as readable text.

11. Data Retention

We may retain account and other information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the service, maintain security, resolve disputes, meet legal requirements, or protect legitimate platform interests.

When information is no longer required, it may be deleted or securely disposed of where appropriate.

12. Your Privacy Choices

Depending on the features available on SkillCircle, users may be able to:

  • View and update account information.
  • Change profile information.
  • Control information displayed on their profile.
  • Request account deletion.
  • Contact SkillCircle about privacy concerns.

13. Account Deletion

You may request deletion of your SkillCircle account using the account deletion feature, if available.

Some information may need to be retained when required for legal, security, fraud-prevention, or legitimate business purposes.

14. Children's Privacy

SkillCircle is intended for users who are legally permitted to use the platform.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable laws.

15. Cookies

SkillCircle may use cookies or similar technologies to maintain login sessions, remember preferences, improve functionality, and understand website usage.

For more information, please see our Cookie Policy.

16. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the platform, features, technologies, or legal requirements change.

The latest version will be published on this page with an updated date.

17. Contact Us

If you have questions, concerns, or requests related to privacy, please contact the SkillCircle administration through the contact method provided on the platform.
