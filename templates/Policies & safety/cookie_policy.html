Cookie Policy

This policy explains how SkillCircle uses cookies and similar technologies.

Last updated: October 2026

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files that a website can store on your device when you visit the website.

They help websites remember information and provide certain features.

2. Why SkillCircle Uses Cookies

SkillCircle may use cookies and similar technologies for purposes such as:

  • Keeping users logged in.
  • Maintaining secure sessions.
  • Remembering selected preferences.
  • Improving website functionality.
  • Understanding basic website usage.
  • Detecting security problems or misuse.

3. Types of Cookies

Essential Cookies

These cookies may be required for important website functions such as login sessions, authentication, security, and navigation.

Preference Cookies

These cookies may remember choices or settings made by the user so the website can provide a more convenient experience.

Analytics Cookies

If analytics services are used, these cookies may help understand how visitors use the website, such as which pages are visited or whether errors occur.

Third-Party Cookies

Some third-party services integrated into SkillCircle may use their own cookies or similar technologies.

4. Session Cookies

SkillCircle may use session cookies to maintain your login session while you use the platform.

These cookies may expire when your session ends or after a defined period of inactivity.

5. How We Use Cookie Information

Cookie-related information may be used to:

  • Authenticate users.
  • Protect accounts and sessions.
  • Maintain website functionality.
  • Remember preferences.
  • Improve website performance.
  • Understand general usage patterns.

6. Managing Cookies

Most modern web browsers allow you to control or delete cookies through their settings.

You can usually choose to block cookies, delete existing cookies, or receive a notification before cookies are stored.

Blocking essential cookies may cause some SkillCircle features, such as login or account sessions, to stop working correctly.

7. Browser Settings

Cookie controls are different for each browser. You can find cookie and privacy settings in the browser's settings or privacy section.

Changing browser settings may affect other websites you use as well.

8. Third-Party Services

SkillCircle may use external services for functions such as hosting, analytics, email, maps, or security.

These services may use cookies or similar technologies according to their own policies.

9. Cookies and Personal Information

Cookies may be associated with information related to your use of SkillCircle.

SkillCircle will handle information collected through cookies according to the applicable Privacy Policy.

10. Changes to This Cookie Policy

This Cookie Policy may be updated when SkillCircle changes its features, technologies, or cookie usage.

The latest version will be published on this page with the updated date.

11. Contact

If you have questions about how SkillCircle uses cookies, contact the SkillCircle administration through the contact method provided on the platform.
