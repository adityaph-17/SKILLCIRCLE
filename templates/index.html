About SkillCircle

A community where learning goes both ways.

SkillCircle is a neighbourhood skill-sharing platform designed to help people exchange knowledge. Instead of treating teaching and learning as separate roles, one profile lets a member list both skills they can teach and skills they want to learn.

The goal is to make practical learning easier to discover, more social, and accessible to people in the community. Members can explore profiles, send learning requests, and arrange sessions based on availability.

Respect and safety

Good learning starts with trust.

SkillCircle can provide tools to report or block users and give admins a way to review concerns. These tools can help, but they cannot guarantee every member or interaction is safe.

✓

Protect your detailsUse approximate location. Do not publish your home address or private information.

✓

Choose a suitable meeting formatMeet online or in a public place when possible, and tell someone you trust about offline plans.

✓

Report concerning behaviourUse reporting and blocking tools where available. Contact local emergency services if you are in immediate danger.
